# Regulatory & Governance Notes

## Explainability
Adverse decisions must be explainable. Record the features and policy rationale behind any
escalation. Model outputs should be calibrated probabilities, not opaque scores.

## Suspicious activity
Patterns consistent with organized fraud or money movement may require internal escalation
to the financial-crimes unit for possible regulatory reporting. Document the basis.

## Fairness & customer impact
False positives create customer friction. Monitor alert rates and outcomes across customer
segments for disparate impact. Maintain an audit trail of automated recommendations and the
human dispositions that followed.

## Model risk management
Models in production require ongoing monitoring: calibration, population stability (PSI),
and out-of-time performance. Material drift triggers revalidation.
